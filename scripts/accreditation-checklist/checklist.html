How to use this checklist An accreditation survey looks for evidence of everyday practice, not intentions. Work through each criterion, tick only what you can evidence today, and note gaps with an owner and a date. Accreditation surveys currently proceed against the 5th edition of the RACGP Standards; the 6th edition (released 26/08/2026) supersedes it and transition arrangements are yet to be announced, so building evidence against the 6th edition now serves both today’s survey and the transition ahead.

Foundations of general practice (F1–F11)

RefCriterionWhat to have readyDone
F1Defining and planning for the practice UPDCurrent plan covering governance, accountability, strategic and operational planning, and risk management
F2Response planningDocumented plans for emergencies and business interruptions; staff know their roles
F3Environmental sustainability and responsibility NEWPractice approach to sustainability and climate resilience (energy, waste, supply choices)
F4Induction, training and supporting performanceInduction records for every role, ongoing training matrix, performance reviews on file
F5Registration and qualifications of practitionersCurrent AHPRA registration, qualifications and indemnity insurance for all practitioners
F6Clinical autonomy of practitionersPractitioners exercise clinical judgement consistent with professional standards
F7Practice team culture, safety and involvementTeam can raise ideas and concerns; meetings documented and acted on
F8Information security UPDSecurity policy, access controls, tested backups and an incident response process (test your systems)
F9Confidentiality and privacy of health and other informationPrivacy policy, signed confidentiality agreements, health records held securely
F10Digital health technologiesDigital tools (My Health Record, electronic prescribing) governed, documented and used safely
F11Artificial intelligence (AI) NEWWhere AI tools are used: patient transparency, human oversight and risk management documented (applies only where AI is in use)

Optional standard: point-of-care testing (PoCT1–PoCT8)

If your practice performs point-of-care testing, the optional PoCT standard also applies: clinical purpose, clinical responsibility, qualifications and training, facilities, performance of tests, data management, quality control and external quality assurance.

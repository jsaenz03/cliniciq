How We Help

AI overlays that slot into your practice's existing systems — start with the work that eats your shift

The Problem

Care Plans Take 30 Minutes

Done properly, a care plan eats about 30 minutes of your day. Even then, details get missed and the documentation piles up — time that should go to the patient in front of you.

ClinicIQ Solution Care Plan Automation

Auto-populated templates using Best Practice data. Generate structured care-plan drafts in seconds — ready for a clinician to review and copy across. Includes prompts for preventative care at opportunistic visits.

Impact: Turn a lengthy documentation task into a quick draft, so you can act on care-plan opportunities while the patient is in the practice. Actual time savings depend on your data, templates and workflow.

The Problem

Poor PIPQI Scores

Allergies not recorded. Hypertensive patients with no BP records. Diabetics with no HbA1c. You're asked to lift the numbers, but "check everyone" sends you chasing the 97% of patients who won't move the needle.

ClinicIQ Solution Targeted PIPQI Improvement

Data-driven targeting using PENCS and analytics. Focus on the 3% of patients affecting your scores instead of spreadsheets across 97%.

Impact: Program changes for PIPQI have been publicly discussed. We can help you focus on the patients most likely to affect your scores; any revenue impact depends on the program rules and your own practice data.

The Problem

Patients Don't Know What They're Eligible For

Health assessments and care plans get poor traction because patients simply don't know what they're entitled to. Meanwhile the feedback box fills with made-up surveys to tick AGPAL boxes.

ClinicIQ Solution Patient Engagement System

QR technology in the waiting room that informs patients of their eligibility in real-time. Same system collects genuine patient feedback that alerts managers to issues needing attention.

Impact: Convert waiting room downtime into service uptake. Patients discover eligible services — the practice captures revenue it was leaving on the table.

The Problem

Tasks Fall Through Cracks

Monthly checks missed. Room restocking happens sometimes, not always. You're the one carrying it all in your head — a heavy mental load, and missed tasks mean missed billings.

ClinicIQ Solution Centralised Task Manager

Centralised task manager accessible to the whole team. Allocate recurring or one-off ad-hoc tasks to specific staff. See who did what-and who hasn't.

Impact: Reduce the chance of tasks being missed by making recurring and ad-hoc work visible to the whole team. This supports timely billing and good documentation — it does not guarantee compliance, which depends on many factors.

The Problem

Stock Management Headaches

The vaccine fridge runs your afternoon: stock runs out mid-clinic, expired vials mean risk and paperwork, and ad-hoc ordering ties up the practice's capital.

ClinicIQ Solution Smart Stock Management

AI-powered stock manager that works alongside Best Practice. Bulk entries using AIR data. Email/SMS alerts on expiring stock and low inventory. Generate insights for smarter ordering.

Impact: Reduce vaccine wastage with expiry alerts and reorder reminders. This supports safer stock handling and smoother appointment flow; actual results depend on usage and your practice workflow.

The Problem

Hours of Repetitive Typing

Billing notes, WorkCover certificates, the same clinical documentation — typed fresh, every shift. And when you need a reliable answer, you have to hunt someone down to ask.

ClinicIQ Solution Policy AI Assistant

Local HTML file with pre-configured note templates. Connects to a RAG AI agent trained on your company policies and FAQs. Ask questions like you're talking to your company SME/expert.

Impact: Reduce repetitive typing with quick reference answers, so staff can stay focused on higher-value work without interrupting colleagues. Actual gains depend on your setup and usage.
